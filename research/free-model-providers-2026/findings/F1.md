# F1: General-purpose inference providers offering free chat, reasoning, coding, vision, embeddings, or reranking APIs.

## Findings

### [1] Z.AI is the strongest new renewable-free candidate: its official price table lists the text/coding/reasoning models GLM-4.7-Flash and GLM-4.5-Flash at zero cost for input, cached input, storage, and output tokens.
- quote: "GLM-4.7-Flash | Free | Free | Free | Free"
- url: https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [2] Z.AI also exposes a genuinely free vision-language API model, GLM-4.6V-Flash, while its other listed vision models remain metered.
- quote: "GLM-4.6V-Flash | Free | Free | Free | Free"
- url: https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [3] Z.AI's image generation, video generation, audio transcription, and built-in web-search APIs are not free, so only its text/reasoning/coding and vision-understanding categories currently fit this repository.
- quote: "GLM-ASR-2512 | $0.03 / MTok"
- url: https://docs.z.ai/guides/overview/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [4] Together AI currently lists Ternary Bonsai 27B serverless text inference at $0.00 per million input and output tokens, making it a candidate free deployment whose duration and rate limits still need live-account verification.
- quote: "Ternary Bonsai 27B | $0.00 | $0.00"
- url: https://www.together.ai/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [5] Jina AI gives each new API key ten million one-time tokens shared across its search-foundation services, so embeddings, reranking, classification, and DeepSearch are trial-credit offerings rather than a renewable free allowance.
- quote: "Every new API key contains ten millions free tokens!"
- url: https://jina.ai/reader/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [6] During Jina AI's free-key trial, both the embedding and reranking APIs permit 100 requests/minute and 100,000 tokens/minute, while DeepSearch exposes an OpenAI-compatible chat-completions endpoint at 50 requests/minute.
- quote: "Embedding API | `https://api.jina.ai/v1/embeddings` | Convert text/images to fixed-length vectors"
- url: https://jina.ai/reader/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [7] Jina Reader is the renewable-free exception at Jina: basic URL-to-text access works without an API key at 20 requests/minute, although it is a document-extraction utility rather than model inference suitable for LiteLLM routing.
- quote: "Reader API is free for basic usage"
- url: https://jina.ai/reader/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [8] Jina AI explicitly supports charges in EUR depending on location, which is positive evidence for individual use from Germany/EU, but its page does not promise EU-hosted inference or GDPR-specific residency.
- quote: "Depending on your location, you may be charged in USD, EUR, or other currencies."
- url: https://jina.ai/reader/
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: medium

### [9] Fireworks AI is not a renewable-free provider: serverless inference starts with only $1 in free credits and embeddings have explicit per-token prices.
- quote: "Get started with $1 in free credits."
- url: https://fireworks.ai/pricing
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

### [10] SambaNova Cloud should no longer be treated as free: its current plan page says even the plan labelled Free requires adding a payment method and purchasing credits before the first request.
- quote: "Add a payment method and purchase credits to run your first requests."
- url: https://cloud.sambanova.ai/plans
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- SiliconFlow official free-model documentation did not surface in the allowed searches, so its frequently claimed free catalog remains unverified.
- Together AI's pricing page contains no general free-tier or free-credit statement; only Ternary Bonsai 27B is priced at zero.
- SambaNova's cached rate-limit documentation still described a Free Tier, but the current live plan page supersedes it and requires purchased credits.
- Fireworks AI offers broad text, vision, embedding, and reranking coverage, but only via a small one-time credit.

## Suggested follow-ups
- Can a newly registered German Z.AI account call all three zero-priced models without balance, payment method, or geographic restriction, and what live RPM/RPD limits apply?
- Does Together AI's Ternary Bonsai 27B return successful API responses to a zero-balance German account, and is its $0 price a promotion or an enduring tier?
- What do Z.AI's and Together AI's terms permit for aggregation through a personal LiteLLM proxy, and where is request data processed?
